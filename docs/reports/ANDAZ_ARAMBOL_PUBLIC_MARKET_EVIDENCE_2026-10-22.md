## Executive assessment
Arambol has a competitive backpacker-accommodation market spanning social dorm hostels, beachfront stays, and hostels with private rooms. Andaz's most defensible *potential* positioning is a social hostel with events and leisure amenities, but every public-facing amenity, policy and price should be reconciled against owner-confirmed data. The defensible short-term priorities are correct listing facts; credible recent photography/reviews; accommodation-category content; structured rate benchmarking by travel date and inclusion; and measurement of inquiries-to-bookings. Market-share, demand, occupancy and ROI are **not measured**.
