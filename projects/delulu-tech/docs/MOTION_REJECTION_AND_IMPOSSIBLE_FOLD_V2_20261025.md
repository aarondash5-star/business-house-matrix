## Owner's direct feedback
> "i didnt really like the animation also no folds nothing just very vanilla not delulu"

## Verified root cause
The production review animation came from `render_motion.py`: it masks the flat frozen SVG by sweeping a left-to-right edge with sine-wave boundary, overlays a violet/light rim Gaussian and lime band, then displays the static white logo. There is no animated physical ribbon topology, changing fold angle, backface, normal, self-occlusion, actual cast shadow, or camera parallax. Frame tests verified timing and color but **not** the promised physical-fold quality. Therefore the previous automated motion "pass" is insufficient.

## Proposed creative replacement — "Impossible Origami / Delulu Fold"
Use a 4-second, silent, 30fps **actual 3D/2.5D folding ribbon performance**, not a flat logo wipe, with the original brand geometry untouched.

- 0.00–0.45s: almost-black stage, a barely visible off-axis violet-lit ribbon edge, obvious physical thickness and shadow.
- 0.45–1.55s: at least **three independent, physically modeled ribbon segments** sweep in, twist around their length axes, and fold across sharp hinged creases. Vary fold angle from >=70° to <=10° with clearly distinct front/back materials; visibly occlude each other. Unnatural but controlled elastic easing and a camera angle that exposes depth.
- 1.55–2.50s: segments weave and cross with Z-depth, cast moving real shadows, a 12°-to-0° camera drift or similar parallax exposes impossible overlapping topology. Moving violet light follows the surfaces, **not** a global full-width band.
- 2.50–2.90s: final fold and tight "impossible" snap; a short spatially localized Volt Lime crackle follows the hinge edge, then immediately recedes.
- 2.90–4.00s: physically flatten and resolve exactly into the **original unmodified frozen SVG wordmark** at final frame (same layout, letterforms and baseline; no generative/logo morphing). Hold motionless in white on ink.
- No confetti, cyberpunk particles, chrome stock logo, glowing typewriter letters, global blur wipes, fake 2D folding generated by opacity or clip-path alone.
