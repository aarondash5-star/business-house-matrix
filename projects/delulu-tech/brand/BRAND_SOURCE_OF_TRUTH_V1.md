## Usage and accessibility

- Use high-contrast Ink/White as the primary visual system. Be minimal at rest and explosive in the reveal.
- Use Volt Lime sparingly for the dominant CTA. Use Ink text on lime, not white text.
- Use Electric Violet for depth/sweeps and rare accents. Soft White text on violet is acceptable.
- Avoid Lime on White (1.08:1) and small Violet on Ink (3.30:1).
- Verified sRGB ratios: Ink on White 17.89:1; Ink on Lime 16.51:1; White on Violet 5.42:1; Gray on Ink 8.71:1; White on Graphite 13.22:1.
- Check real overlays, gradients, hover states and focus indications against WCAG 2.2. Do not communicate state by color alone.
